# open-todo

**Before doing anything else, read `docs/HANDOVER.md` — start at its §0.**

That document is the project's memory: purpose, what was already investigated
(and what was investigated and rejected), the decisions taken with their
rationale, and the decisions still open.

Two standing rules:

- **Never `git push` or open a PR** without the owner's explicit go-ahead in the
  current session.
- **No Todoist code, bundles, icons, fonts, or logos** enter this repository.
  See §6 of the handover.
